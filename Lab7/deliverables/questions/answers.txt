CAN THE RESOURCE USE BE REDUCED FURTHER?
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Yes. Two cheap levers.

1. Take softmax out of the hardware. Return the 10 logits and do exp/div
   on the CPU or in the testbench. Argmax stays the same. The float
   exp/div/add IP is 9 of the 13 DSPs and about 5100 of the 5800 LUTs,
   and it is also the timing bottleneck.
   quantized fx<20,6> with softmax removed:
       LUT  5788 -> 673
       FF   2185 -> 466
       DSP    13 -> 4
       BRAM    6 unchanged
       lat  8645 -> 8326 cyc
       est. clk 8.51 ns -> 4.20 ns

2. Narrower word. fx<20,6> -> fx<16,6> still matches Python
   (max dP about 5e-3, under the 1e-2 limit):
       LUT  5788 -> 5674
       FF   2185 -> 2129
       BRAM    6 -> 3
       DSP and latency unchanged

Both levers combine. Rolled loops already use one shared MAC unit per
layer, so nothing else can be cut without losing accuracy. The committed
projects keep fx<20,6> and hardware softmax so the testbench stays
self-checking. Switching is a one-line interface change.


CAN THE CLOCK PERIOD BE IMPROVED? WHAT HAPPENS TO PERFORMANCE?
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Fixed-point (quantized and pruned). Over-constrained at 10 ns, est 8.51.
A tighter constraint works and costs little.

  target   est.clk   lat[cyc]   FF     LUT    lat[us]
  10 ns    8.51 ns   8645       2185   5788   86.5
   7 ns    6.04 ns   8695       2349   5842   60.9
   5 ns    4.35 ns   8866       2952   6019   44.3

Cycle count barely moves because the loops are not pipelined, so run time
drops almost linearly with the period. About 2x faster at 5 ns for about
35 percent more FF. With softmax removed the datapath closes at 4.2 ns,
so it could run near a 4 ns clock.

Float (mlp_FP). Already at the limit at 10 ns, est 9.99. Asking for less
does not help.
   7 ns gives est 7.50 ns (misses), latency 37375 -> 41519
   5 ns gives est 5.67 ns (misses), latency 62230
The float adder and multiplier IP latency sets the floor. A tighter
target only adds pipeline stages, so more FF and more cycles, no real
speed-up.


HOW DO THE 3 DESIGNS DIFFER IN FPGA RESOURCE UTILIZATION?
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Float uses the fewest LUT and FF but the most BRAM. Its arithmetic is
  hardened float IP, DSP-heavy and LUT-light, and the 32-bit weight
  tables need 11 BRAM18.

- Quantized uses about 1.6x the LUT of float and half the BRAM. The
  ap_fixed multiply and add sit in fabric (LUT), and the 20-bit weight
  ROMs need only 6 BRAM18. DSP is about the same, 12 against 13, both
  dominated by the shared float softmax.

- Pruned equals quantized. LUT 5803 against 5788, FF 2201 against 2185,
  DSP 13, BRAM 5 against 6. Pruning zeroed about 79 percent of the
  weights but they are still stored and still multiplied by the dense
  MAC, so no logic is saved. The one BRAM difference is just ROM packing.
  A real saving would need a sparse or skip-zero datapath, which this
  code does not have.

Utilisation is small either way, at most 8 percent LUT, 3 percent DSP,
3 percent BRAM.


HOW DO THE 3 DESIGNS DIFFER IN PERFORMANCE?
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Latency. Fixed-point is about 4.3x faster than float, 8645 against
  37375 cycles. A float add takes about 9 cycles, so each iteration of
  the rolled neuron_mac loop takes 9 cycles (784 x 9 x 5 = 35300 for
  layer 1 alone). The ap_fixed add is 1 to 2 cycles, so the same loop is
  about 4x shorter.

- Clock. Fixed-point also closes faster, 8.5 ns against 10.0 ns at the
  same target, and it still meets 5 ns while float cannot beat 10 ns.

- Time per inference. Float about 374 us. Quantized and pruned about
  86 us at 10 ns and about 44 us at 5 ns. Pruned and quantized are
  identical, same interval, same cycles, same Fmax.

- All three are single-call and not pipelined (ap_ctrl_hs, interval
  equals latency), so throughput is 1 over latency. Pipelining
  neuron_mac to interval 1 would cut latency to about 4 to 5k cycles but
  roughly doubles LUT and FF and needs the weight ROMs partitioned.
